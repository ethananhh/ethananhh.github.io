TubeForge.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

TubeForge ("the app") is a personal, self-hosted tool. It is installed and run by the channel owner on their own computer or server. There is no TubeForge company server: we, the developers, never receive your data.

1. Data the app accesses

When you connect your Google account, you grant the app these permissions:

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos you have approved to your channel.
  • youtube.readonly — to read basic information about your channel and videos.
  • yt-analytics.readonly and yt-analytics-monetary.readonly — to read your channel's analytics (views, watch time, subscribers, estimated revenue).

2. How the data is used

  • Only to upload your videos and to display your own channel's statistics on your private dashboard.
  • No advertising, profiling, or selling of data. Data is not used to train AI models.

3. Storage

  • Your OAuth refresh token is stored in a local configuration file (.env) on the machine running the app.
  • Analytics numbers and video metadata are stored in a local database on that same machine.
  • You can delete all data at any time by deleting the app's database and configuration file.

4. Sharing

Google user data is not shared with anyone. To create scripts, the app sends the topic and channel settings you write (never your Google data) to the AI provider you configure (Anthropic). Text-to-speech and stock-footage services receive only the script text and search keywords.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

TubeForge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/ethananhh/tube-forge/issues.
