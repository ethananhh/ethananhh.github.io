TubeForge.

AI YouTube content studio

TubeForge is a personal, self-hosted application. It helps one creator draft video scripts with AI, generate a voice-over, assemble a video from licensed stock footage, and upload it to their own YouTube channel. It also shows that channel's analytics on a private dashboard.

How it uses Google / YouTube data

  • Upload videos you approve to your own channel (YouTube Data API).
  • Read your channel's analytics — views, watch time, subscribers, estimated revenue — to show them on your dashboard (YouTube Analytics API).
  • Data stays on the computer/server where you run TubeForge. It is never sold or shared.
